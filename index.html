TASK & DIAGRAM

IELTS WRITING TASK 1

The diagram below shows the natural and human-related processes involved in the sulphur cycle, from the release of sulphur into the atmosphere to its deposition, uptake by plants and animals, decomposition, and long-term storage.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

⏱ About 20 minutes✍ At least 150 words◉ Process diagram
THE SULPHUR CYCLE · PROCESS MAP
01🌋 Release

Sulphur enters the atmosphere, mainly as SO₂.

02☁ Transformation

SO₂ reacts with oxygen and water.

03🌧 Deposition

Compounds return through wet and dry deposition.

04🌱 Plant Uptake

Plants absorb sulphate ions.

05🐾 Consumption

Animals obtain sulphur through the food chain.

06🍂 Decomposition

Microorganisms break down organic material.

07🌊 Runoff

Sulphates move through rivers to the ocean.

08🪨 Storage

Sulphur is stored in crust and oceanic sediments.

Quick Scan

Before writing, identify: starting point · major transformation · biological stages · final storage.
