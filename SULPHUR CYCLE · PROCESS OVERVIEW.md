# 🧪 SULPHUR CYCLE · PROCESS OVERVIEW
| **Stage** | **Process / Key Event**           | **What Happens?**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | **Result / Outcome**                       |
| :-------: | --------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
|   **01**  | 🌋 **Sulphur Release**            | Sulphur enters the atmosphere mainly as **sulphur dioxide (SO₂)**. Natural sources include **volcanic activity**, rocks and the ocean, while human activities such as **burning fossil fuels** and **metal processing** also release substantial amounts. | **Sulphur enters the atmosphere**          |
|   **02**  | ☁️ **Atmospheric Transformation** | Once airborne, **sulphur dioxide reacts with oxygen and water**, producing **sulphate salts and sulphuric acid**.                                                                                                                                         | **Sulphur is chemically transformed**      |
|   **03**  | 🌧️ **Deposition**                | Sulphate compounds return to the Earth's surface through **wet and dry deposition**.                                                                                                                                                                      | **Sulphur returns to land and water**      |
|   **04**  | 🌱 **Plant Uptake**               | Plants absorb **sulphate ions** from the environment and incorporate sulphur into **proteins**.                                                                                                                                                           | **Sulphur enters the food chain**          |
|   **05**  | 🐾 **Animal Consumption**         | Animals consume plants and obtain sulphur through the **food chain**.                                                                                                                                                                                     | **Sulphur is transferred to animals**      |
|   **06**  | 🍂 **Decomposition & Recycling**  | When plants and animals die, **microorganisms break down organic material**, releasing sulphur compounds back into the **soil and water**.                                                                                                                | **Sulphur is recycled through ecosystems** |
|   **07**  | 🌊 **Runoff & Ocean Transfer**    | Some sulphates are transported through **runoff into rivers** and eventually reach the **ocean**.                                                                                                                                                         | **Sulphur moves into aquatic systems**     |
|   **08**  | 🪨 **Long-Term Storage**          | A portion of sulphur becomes stored in the **Earth's crust and oceanic sediments**.                                                                                                                                                                       | **Long-term sulphur storage**              |


# 🎯 OVERALL SUMMARY

> Sulphur enters the atmosphere through natural and human activities, undergoes chemical transformation, returns to the Earth's surface through deposition, and is subsequently absorbed, transferred, recycled and eventually stored in geological and oceanic reservoirs.

# 🧭 THE CYCLE IN ONE LINE

> Release → Transformation → Deposition → Uptake → Consumption → Decomposition → Runoff → Storage → ♻️ Recycling
